📲 Put it on your home screen

iPhone or iPad: open this page in Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. It opens full screen, like a real app.

Android: in Chrome, tap ⋮, then Add to Home screen.

You get a 🛰️ icon on your home screen. It only works on your home Wi-Fi, because that's where your Pi is.
